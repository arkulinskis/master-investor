How you learn here

The same loop for a first-time saver and a working trader. It takes five to ten minutes a day.

  1. Read one short lesson.Plain words, one worked example with real-looking numbers, and the key terms in the margin.
  2. Answer before you look.Each question hides its answer. Think it through, press Reveal, then say honestly whether you knew it.
  3. Come back when it's due.Cards you know return in days, then weeks. Cards you missed return tomorrow. That spacing is what makes it stick.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Every lesson, card, calculator and the library are free. An account is free too, and only needed to save your progress and review schedule.

Is this financial advice?

No. Master Investor teaches how investing works and how professionals think. It never tells you what to buy or sell, and the examples use made-up companies.

I'm a complete beginner. Where do I start?

With Foundations: what investing is, compounding, risk, fees and how a trade happens. No maths beyond percentages.

I already trade. Is there anything for me?

The Trading, Portfolio and Quant tracks go deep: expectancy and risk of ruin, microstructure, factor models, backtest overfitting, cointegration, execution and Black-Scholes. Take the placement check to skip what you know.

How does the reveal-and-review work?

You see a question, answer it in your head, reveal, then mark Got it or Not yet. Cards you know come back after 1, 3, 7, 16 and 35 days; cards you miss come back sooner.

Who can see my progress?

Only you. We store your finished lessons and card reviews to schedule them, nothing else. The Privacy & Terms page has the details.
